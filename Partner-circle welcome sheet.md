# Welcome to the Partner Circle

*The Big Tech Group — Global Governance Compendium, Partners Pre-Release*

## The circle

The partner circle is the committed ring of partners standing behind the
TBTG Global Governance Compendium: those who have made the Declaration of
Intent, stood in the Circle of Fealty, and ratified the work. The circle
exists to steward the compendium from draft to law — to read it closely,
to challenge it honestly, and to carry it into the rooms where it matters.

Membership here is a trust, not a title. What is shared in the circle
stays in the circle until the compendium is cleared for public release.

## The pre-release gate

What you hold is a **pre-release draft** — the complete 21 works, shared
with partners before anyone else, and before the public.

- **Partners only.** Circulation is limited to the committed circle.
  No forwarding, no public posting, no excerpts outside the circle.
- **Draft, not instrument.** The compendium has not yet been reviewed by
  licensed counsel. Nothing here is published, filed, or relied upon as a
  legal instrument.
- **Counsel is the next gate.** Copyright-assignment language in these
  works has legal effect. Public release follows counsel clearance —
  not before.

If you are ever unsure whether something may be shared, ask first. The
duty of loyalty runs both ways.

## Who benefits

The circle is built to benefit its people: the partners, their staff,
and their families. The work we do here — the reading, the counsel
review, the rooms we carry it into — accrues to the circle first. Bring
people in with that in mind: every new partner should be someone whose
presence makes the circle, and everyone it supports, stronger.

## What to do now

1. Read the compendium — `tbtg-governance-compendium.md`, or the styled
   `tbtg-governance-compendium.html` for the full reading experience.
2. Note what needs counsel's eyes. Questions of IP, assignment, and
   enforceability go on the list for licensed review.
3. Hold the gate. Partners only, until counsel clears it.
