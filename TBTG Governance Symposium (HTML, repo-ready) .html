I. UNIFIED POLICY & GOVERNANCE ARCHITECTURE

A Codified Corporate Framework Rooted in Jus Gentium, Eng Legis, and Global Norms

1. TERMS OF USE

Regula Fundamentalis Operandi

1.1 Nature of Agreement. By accessing any TBTG platform, system, or service, the user enters as eng legis — a lawful participant — and consents to these Terms of Use. This agreement is governed by pacta sunt servanda: agreements must be honored.

1.2 Scope of Authorized Use. Users may engage with TBTG systems solely for legitimate, ethical, and permitted purposes. Unauthorized access, interference, or exploitation constitutes a violation recognized under global cyber-crime jurisprudence, including the reasoning in United States v. Morris, 928 F.2d 504 (2d Cir. 1991), and parallel international authorities.

1.3 Intellectual Property Rights. All content, frameworks, systems, and materials are the proprietary property of The Big Tech Group (TBTG) and its partners, protected under the Berne Convention (1886), the TRIPS Agreement (1994), and the WIPO Copyright Treaty (1996). Reproduction, distribution, or the creation of derivative works requires prior written authorization.

1.4 User Conduct Obligations. Users shall uphold bona fides — good faith — refrain from harmful conduct, and respect confidentiality. Conduct contrary to public order (ordre public) or international norms may result in immediate termination of access.

1.5 Disclaimers & Limitations. TBTG disclaims liability for indirect or consequential damages, consistent with global commercial standards and the interpretations under the UNCITRAL Model Law on Electronic Commerce (1996).

1.6 Termination of Access. TBTG reserves the right to suspend or terminate access for violations of law, ethics, or policy.

2. PRIVACY POLICY

Lex Datorum et Protectio Personarum

2.1 Commitment to Data Protection. TBTG adheres to global privacy norms, including the GDPR (EU Regulation 2016/679), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), the OECD Privacy Guidelines (1980), and the APEC Privacy Framework (2004). Personal data is treated as a protected interest, in keeping with the peremptory principles of dignity and autonomy.

2.2 Data Collection Principles. Data is collected only for legitimate operational, analytical, and security purposes, following finis determinatus — purpose limitation.

2.3 Data Usage. Data is used strictly within the bounds of operational necessity. No unauthorized sale, sharing, or transfer shall occur.

2.4 User Rights. Users retain rights of access, correction, deletion, and portability, aligned with GDPR Articles 12–23 and parallel global statutes.

2.5 Security Measures. TBTG employs safeguards consistent with ISO/IEC 27001, ensuring confidentiality, integrity, and availability.

2.6 Data Retention. Data is retained only as long as necessary for lawful and operational purposes.

3. AI TERMS OF GOVERNANCE

Machinae Sapientes et Responsabilitas

3.1 Ethical Foundations. TBTG's AI systems operate under fairness, transparency, and accountability, aligned with the UNESCO AI Ethics Recommendation (2021), the OECD AI Principles (2019), and the EU AI Act (2024).

3.2 Nature of AI Outputs. AI outputs are probabilistic and informational. They do not constitute legal, medical, or professional advice.

3.3 Responsible Use. Users shall not employ AI systems for unlawful surveillance, discriminatory profiling, manipulation or coercion, or automated decision-making without human oversight where the law requires it.

3.4 Data Handling in AI Systems. AI systems process data in compliance with global privacy law and lex technica standards.

3.5 Accountability & Oversight. High-risk AI systems maintain human-in-the-loop governance, consistent with Article 14 of the EU AI Act.

4. LEGAL NOTICE

Lex Contractus et Jurisdictio Universalis

4.1 Governing Law. This framework is interpreted under the principles of international commercial law, including the UNCITRAL Model Laws, the Restatement (Second) of Conflict of Laws, and jus gentium — the law of nations.
